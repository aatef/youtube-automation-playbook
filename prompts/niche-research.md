## 1. Niche brainstorm

```
You are a YouTube strategist specialising in faceless channels.

Suggest 15 faceless YouTube niches for a creator with these constraints:
- Target audience country: {US / UK / global English}
- Production capacity: {e.g. 3 long-form videos per week, AI voice + stock/AI B-roll}
- Interests / expertise: {e.g. technology, business history}
- Budget per video: {e.g. $20}

For each niche give: a narrower sub-niche angle, estimated RPM range (state it's an estimate),
competition level, 3 example video titles, and the main risk under YouTube's
inauthentic-content policy. Return a table, then recommend your top 3 with reasons.
```

## 2. Outlier analysis (paste data in)

```
Below is a list of videos from competitor channels in the {niche} niche, with views and
channel average views. Identify the outliers (5× or more above channel average).

For each outlier explain:
1. The title formula (abstract it into a reusable template)
2. The likely thumbnail concept
3. The emotional trigger (curiosity, fear, aspiration, etc.)
4. Why it probably outperformed

Then give me 10 ORIGINAL video ideas for my channel that use the same mechanics
but cover different topics. Do not copy titles.

DATA:
{paste: title | views | channel avg views}
```

## 3. 50-idea backlog

```
My channel: {channel description, audience, format}.
Generate 50 video ideas grouped into 5 content pillars. For each idea give:
working title | search-driven or browse-driven | evergreen or trending | difficulty (1-5).
Prioritise ideas that a new channel can rank for. No duplicates or near-duplicates.
```

## 4. Niche validation

```
I'm considering the niche: {niche}, targeting {audience}.
Play devil's advocate. Give me the 7 strongest reasons this niche could fail for a new
faceless channel in 2026 (saturation, RPM, policy risk, production difficulty, topic depth).
Then tell me what evidence I should look for on YouTube to prove or disprove each risk.
```
