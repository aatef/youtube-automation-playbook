# Monetization Criteria (YouTube Partner Program)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

> Last checked: November 2026. YouTube changes these rules regularly. Always confirm in **YouTube Studio → Earn** and the official YPP help pages.

---

## ⚠️ The big one: thresholds double on 1 February 2027

YouTube announced on 10 Aug 2026 that **new applicants** for ad revenue need double the current watch hours or Shorts views. Channels already in YPP are grandfathered.

| Requirement | Until 31 Jan 2027 | From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants) |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,000 | 1,000 (unchanged) |
| Long-form path | 4,000 qualified watch hours / 12 months | **8,000** qualified watch hours / 365 days |
| Shorts path | 10M qualified Shorts views / 90 days | **20M** qualified Shorts views / 90 days |

**What this means for us:** a channel started now will almost certainly apply *after* February 2027. **Plan for 8,000 hours.** That pushes us towards long-form content with good retention, not Shorts-only.

## The two tiers

### Tier 1: Fan Funding (expanded YPP, where available)
- 500 subscribers
- 3 public uploads in the last 90 days
- 3,000 qualified watch hours / 12 months **or** 3M qualified Shorts views / 90 days
- Unlocks: memberships, Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers, Shopping. **No ad revenue.**
- These thresholds are not changing in 2027.

### Tier 2: Full monetisation
- 1,000 subscribers + watch-hours **or** Shorts path (see table above)
- Unlocks: ad revenue on long-form and Shorts, YouTube Premium revenue, plus everything in Tier 1.

## What counts as "qualified"

| Counts ✅ | Does NOT count ❌ |
|---|---|
| Watch time on **public** long-form videos | Private, unlisted or deleted videos |
| Engaged views on **public** Shorts in the Shorts feed | Views from ads (paid promotion) |
| | Shorts watch time towards the long-form hours |
| | Live streams that weren't archived |

**The two paths never combine.** 5,000 hours plus 15M Shorts views qualifies for neither. You must complete one path on its own.

## Universal requirements (both tiers)
- Follow YouTube's monetisation policies, Community Guidelines, Terms of Service and the AdSense programme policies
- **No active Community Guidelines strikes**
- 2-Step Verification on the Google account
- Live in a country where YPP is available (Pakistan is eligible)
- One linked AdSense account
- Pass a **human review** of the channel's overall content against the inauthentic-content and reused-content policies. See [AI Usage](ai-usage.md).

## Staying monetised
- Accept updated YPP terms when prompted (existing partners have until 31 Jan 2027 to accept the latest terms)
- Stay active: keep uploading, or keep a baseline of watch hours or Shorts views
- One policy problem can be reviewed channel-wide. Reviewers look at the channel's **overall pattern**, not just single videos.

## Pakistan-specific notes
- AdSense pays to a Pakistani bank account by wire transfer once you reach the payout threshold. Set up tax info (US tax form) early to avoid excess withholding.
- RPM depends on **where viewers are**, not where you are. Content aimed at US, UK, Canadian or Australian audiences earns much more per 1,000 views than content aimed at South Asian audiences.

## Before you apply: checklist
- [ ] Threshold met on **one** path
- [ ] No active strikes
- [ ] 2SV on
- [ ] Every video has an original script, voice or editorial angle
- [ ] No reused clips without transformation
- [ ] AI disclosure set correctly on realistic synthetic content
- [ ] Channel looks like a real publication: About section, consistent branding, playlists
