# Free Video Generators

[← Back to README](../README.md)

> Free tiers change often. These allowances were reported in late 2026, so check each vendor's pricing page before relying on them.

### AI video generation (text/image → video)
| Tool | Free allowance | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kling AI** | Daily credits (≈ 1 short clip/day) | Cinematic, realistic B-roll | 5-second clips; credits go fast |
| **Dreamina** (CapCut) | Daily credits | Repeat text/image-to-video experiments | Terms vary by model and region |
| **Krea** | 100 compute units/day | Trying several video models in one place | Limited access to the top models |
| **PixVerse** | Daily credits (resets 00:00 UTC) | Effect-driven Shorts | 20–60 credits per generation |
| **Pika** | 80 credits/month | Short effects and transformations | Mostly 480p on free |
| **Luma Dream Machine** | Limited free credits | Quick concept tests | Model and export limits |
| **Hailuo AI** | Free credits | Realistic people and expressive motion | Queue times on free |
| **Wan 2.x** (open source) | Unlimited if run locally | High volume with no credit limits | Needs a capable GPU |

### Faceless video assembly (script → full video)
| Tool | Free allowance | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **InVideo AI** | 10 AI minutes/week, 4 exports/week, 720p | Watermark; **no commercial use** on free |

### Free editors (to finish everything)
| Tool | Notes |
|---|---|
| **CapCut** | Fast editing, captions, templates. Good for Shorts. |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Professional-grade free editor for long-form. |

### Rules for using free tiers on a monetised channel
1. **Check commercial-use rights.** Several free tiers ban commercial use, and a monetised channel counts as commercial.
2. **No watermarks in final uploads.** They look cheap and can signal reused content.
3. **Use AI clips as B-roll, not the whole video.** Original script, voice and editing are what keep you clear of the inauthentic-content policy.
4. **Stack daily credits.** Rotate between tools with daily refills (Kling, Dreamina, Krea, PixVerse) to build a library of clips.
