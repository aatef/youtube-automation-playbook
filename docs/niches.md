# Popular Niches for Faceless / Automation Channels

[← Back to README](../README.md)

> RPM ranges below are **rough creator-reported estimates for mostly US audiences (2026)**. Real numbers vary a lot with audience country, season, video length and format. Shorts RPM is far lower (roughly $0.04–$0.40).

---

## High-RPM niches

| Niche | Est. long-form RPM | Faceless fit | Competition | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal finance & investing** | $15–35 | High | High | Top earner. ⚠️ No AI-presenter advice; keep it educational, with a human voice and fact-checking |
| **Insurance / legal explainers** | $15–40+ | High | Medium | Very high CPM, but needs accuracy |
| **Make money online / side hustles** | $12–20 | High | Very high | Saturated; needs a unique angle |
| **Software / SaaS / AI tool tutorials** | $10–25 | Very high | Medium | Screen recordings + voice. Strong affiliate income too |
| **Business case studies** | $8–20 | Very high | Medium | e.g. "Why X company failed." Story-driven |
| **Real estate** | $10–16 | High | Medium | Market explainers, investing basics |
| **Digital marketing** | $12–18 | High | Medium | Tutorials, strategy breakdowns |

## High-volume / storytelling niches

| Niche | Est. RPM | Faceless fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **History documentaries** (animated) | $4–10 | Very high | Evergreen; rewards research and narration |
| **Horror / mystery stories** | $3–8 | Very high | Story templates scale well; watch for reused-content patterns |
| **Betrayal / revenge / drama narratives** | $5–13 | Very high | Currently hot; high risk of looking templated |
| **Science & space explainers** | $5–12 | Very high | Strong AI B-roll fit |
| **Geography / "how countries work"** | $5–10 | Very high | Maps + data visualisation |
| **Psychology & self-improvement** | $5–12 | High | Saturated; needs a distinct voice |
| **Top-10 / list videos** | $3–8 | Very high | Easy to systemise; easy to fall into "inauthentic" territory |

## Niches to avoid (or be very careful with)
- ❌ **Compilations / reaction / clip channels:** reused content, not monetisable
- ❌ **Movie / TV recaps using actual footage:** copyright strikes
- ❌ **Celebrity gossip with AI voices or faces:** impersonation and defamation risk
- ❌ **Kids' content:** "Made for kids" rules mean no personalised ads and much lower RPM
- ⚠️ **Health / medical advice:** high scrutiny; never with AI presenters

## How to pick: score each candidate niche

| Criterion | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| Demand (search + outlier videos exist) | |
| RPM / affiliate potential | |
| Can we produce it **originally** with our pipeline? | |
| Can we make 100+ videos without running out of topics? | |
| Do we personally find it interesting enough to keep going for 12 months? | |

**Pick the highest total, not the highest RPM.** Then go narrower: "personal finance" → "personal finance for first-job Gen Z in the US".

## Validation steps
1. Find **5–10 channels under 1 year old** in the niche with outlier videos. That proves new channels can break in.
2. List their top 10 videos → extract title formulas and thumbnail styles.
3. Write **20 video ideas**. If it's a struggle, drop the niche.
4. Make 3 test videos before committing.

See [prompts/niche-research.md](../prompts/niche-research.md) for AI prompts that speed this up.
