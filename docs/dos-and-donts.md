# Dos and Don'ts of YouTube (Automation Edition)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

---

## ✅ Do

### Strategy
- **Pick one niche and stay in it** for at least 50 videos. Consistency trains the algorithm and the audience.
- **Study outliers.** Find videos with 5–10× their channel's average views and break down why they worked.
- **Target high-RPM audiences** (US/UK/CA/AU) with English content if revenue is the goal.
- **Plan for the 8,000-hour threshold** (from Feb 2027). Long-form with strong retention matters more than ever.
- **Expect 8–18 months** before meaningful income. Budget time and money for that.

### Content
- **Write the title and thumbnail first**, then the script. If you can't package it, don't make it.
- **Hook in the first 30 seconds:** deliver on the promise in the title right away.
- **Give each video an original angle**: research, data, a story, or analysis no one else has.
- **Fact-check everything** an AI wrote.
- **Use chapters, end screens and playlists** to increase session time.

### Operations
- **Write SOPs** for every step (research, script, voice, edit, thumbnail, upload).
- **Keep a licence log** for every asset in every video.
- **Set AI disclosure correctly** on every upload.
- **Turn on 2-Step Verification** and keep channel access with as few people as possible (use Brand Account roles, never share passwords).
- **Review analytics weekly:** CTR, average view duration, retention graph, traffic sources.

## ❌ Don't

### Policy killers
- **Don't mass-produce templated AI videos.** This is the #1 cause of demonetisation for automation channels.
- **Don't reupload or compile** other people's content.
- **Don't use copyrighted music**, movie/TV clips or sports footage.
- **Don't impersonate real people** with AI voices or faces.
- **Don't make AI-presenter health or finance advice videos.** These are explicitly not monetisable.
- **Don't buy subscribers, views or watch hours**, or join sub-for-sub groups. This is fake engagement and can get the channel terminated.
- **Don't use misleading thumbnails or titles** that the video doesn't deliver on.

### Strategy mistakes
- **Don't chase "highest RPM" niches you can't produce well.** A $20 RPM with 200 views earns less than a $4 RPM with 50,000 views.
- **Don't switch niches every few weeks.**
- **Don't quit at month 3.** That's when most people give up, just before the algorithm starts testing the channel properly.
- **Don't buy courses or tools** until you've finished the free material and published consistently.
- **Don't buy a "monetised channel"** from a marketplace. It breaks YouTube's terms and is frequently a scam.

### Security
- **Don't click "sponsorship" emails with download links.** Channel-hijacking malware targets creators.
- **Don't give editors or VAs your Google password.** Use Studio permissions instead.

## Quick pre-upload checklist
- [ ] Title + thumbnail tested (2–3 variants)
- [ ] Hook delivers the title's promise in the first 30 s
- [ ] Script human-edited and fact-checked
- [ ] All assets licensed and logged
- [ ] No watermarks
- [ ] AI disclosure set correctly
- [ ] Description, chapters, tags, end screen, playlist
- [ ] "Made for kids" set correctly
