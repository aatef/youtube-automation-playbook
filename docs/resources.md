## 💡 Free-tier tips
1. **Stack daily credits:** rotate between AI tools that refresh daily.
2. **Check commercial use:** a monetised channel counts as commercial.
3. **No watermarks** in final uploads.
4. **Upgrade only when a tool is your bottleneck,** and only once the channel earns.
